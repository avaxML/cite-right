# Upgrade from 0.4.0 to 0.5.0

Recheck any tests or UI that depend on exact span statuses, citation counts,
or serialized component keys.

## Span status can change

- Reordered wording can move from `unsupported` to `partial` or `supported`
  when the citation meets the relevant coverage and support checks. Shared
  punctuation alone no longer admits a citation, which can leave the span
  `unsupported`.
- Faithful restatements can move from `partial` to `supported` when headings,
  capitalization, UI labels, or unit codes previously triggered an entity
  check. On the default Rust path, equivalent amounts such as `1,200` and
  `1200` can also move up to `supported`.
- Previously `supported` spans can move to `partial` when matching clauses
  reverse `before` and `after`, names change in corresponding roles, a click,
  press, or tap targets a different control, or an amount changes in value,
  sign, percentage marker, explicit currency unit, label, or direction.

## Citation output

`Citation.components["content_coverage"]` is the fraction of answer content
tokens found in the full candidate passage, counting repeated occurrences.
Update exact-key checks and stored snapshots to include it.

On a `supported` span, secondaries that fail the primary support checks are
eligible for `retrieval_support`, subject to `max_retrieval_support`. A
lower-ranked supporting citation can take the freed slot under `top_k` or
`max_citations_per_source`. The primary citation determines span status.

## Opt-in settings

- `CitationConfig(report_possible_source=True)` adds `status_reason` and
  `contradiction_checks`. A qualifying `partial` or `unsupported` span can also
  get a `possible_source` with a passage range and retrieval score. Treat it
  as a hint and verify the passage before citing it.
- `CitationConfig(term_aliases=(("clamp", "bracket"),))` declares equivalent
  single words for retrieval and alignment with `SimpleTokenizer`. With
  aliases enabled, `supported` also requires full content coverage in the
  candidate passage. Evidence and offsets still refer to the original source.

## Internal Rust bindings

The private `cite_right._core` prepare binding now requires the keyword-only
`normalize_numbers`, `normalize_percent`, and `normalize_currency` flags. Both
Rust citation builders require `query_stopword_ids` and accept optional
`alias_remap`. In `rust_build_citations_fast`, both are keyword-only and
`query_stopword_ids` is newly required. Public calls to `align_citations` and
`PreparedCitationCorpus` need no changes for these internal bindings. See
[Rust acceleration](../advanced/rust-acceleration.md).
